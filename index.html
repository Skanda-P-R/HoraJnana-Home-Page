Key Capabilities

Everything You Need for Vedic Horary

Crafted specifically for Vedic astrologers, researchers, and practitioners who require exact celestial timings and zero latency.

Live Planetary Hora Countdown

Real-time tracking of the active Hora with remaining seconds and minutes. Automatically triggers updates when the ruling planetary hour transitions.

Complete 5 Panchanga Limbs

Exact ending moments for Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, and Vara. Accompanied by full Vedic calendar elements: Samvatsara, Ayana, Rutu, Masa, and Paksha.

Interactive Kundali Engine

Toggle between South Indian, North Indian, and East Indian visual chart conventions. Explore planetary degrees, Moon Nakshatra, and retrograde indicators.

3-Level Dasha Hierarchy

Hierarchical drill-down through Vimshottari Dasha periods: Mahadasha, Antardasha, and Pratyantardasha, with live balance calculations and breadcrumb navigation.

Precise Muhurta Periods

Accurate calculations for Rahu Kalam, Yamaganda, Gulika, and Abhijit Muhurta based on dynamic sunrise and sunset coordinates for the user's specific location.

Bilingual English & Kannada

Complete high-fidelity localization. Toggle instantly between English and Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ) with authentic Vedic terminology rendered natively in Noto Sans Kannada.

Web Crypto API & AES-GCM

Kundali birth files are encrypted on the client side using AES-GCM 256-bit keys. Your personal birth dates, times, and coordinates are never stored unencrypted.

Cross-Platform Sync
